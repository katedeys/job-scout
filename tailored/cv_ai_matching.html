AI Job-Matching Agent — personal productOct 2026 – now
Built with Claude Code; runs daily for my own job search
  • Retrieval: pulls openings from ~200 company career sites across 9 ATS (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Teamtailor, Workable and others), LinkedIn alerts and Telegram channels; dedupes and keeps only new postings.
